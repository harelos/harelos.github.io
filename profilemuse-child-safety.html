Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Our commitment

ProfileMuse: AI Dating Photos is an adult-only creative photo service from TigerBrands Global. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). ProfileMuse: AI Dating Photos must never be used to create, upload, request, store, distribute, or promote content that sexually exploits, abuses, endangers, or depicts a minor in a sexual context.

Age and prohibited content

  • ProfileMuse is only for people who are 18 years of age or older.
  • Users may not upload or generate images of minors or use the service to sexualize, groom, exploit, threaten, or endanger a child.
  • Users may not upload another person’s photo without consent, create deepfakes, or create content intended to harass, defame, or harm someone.
  • Any attempt to create or distribute CSAE or CSAM is prohibited and may result in blocking, removal, account termination, and reporting to the appropriate authorities where required or appropriate.

Reporting inside ProfileMuse

Users can report a generated image from the in-app report control shown on the image screen. Reports are recorded for moderation review. If you cannot access the app, report a child-safety concern by email using the contact details below. Include enough information for us to locate the content, but do not send illegal imagery by email.

How we respond

ProfileMuse uses image-safety moderation on submitted and generated images and maintains a moderation queue for user reports. When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAE or CSAM, we will take appropriate action under these standards and applicable law. This may include restricting access, removing content, preserving relevant information when lawful and necessary, terminating accounts, and reporting to the appropriate child-safety or law-enforcement authority.

Child safety point of contact

The TigerBrands Global Child Safety Contact Team is available to receive child-safety reports and requests from Google Play. Contact support@tigerbrandsglobal.com with the subject line “ProfileMuse child safety report”.

These standards supplement the ProfileMuse Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
