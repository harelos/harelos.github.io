About the “tax” bullshit: Paraguay’s general IVA rate is 10%. On most of these offers, the platform math is exactly room price + 10%. But an OTA can put other commissions/fees inside a label like “taxes & fees,” and one feed can also be wrong. Hotel Rural Jerutì is the clearest example: one supplier feed showed $24.30 + $24.43 in “taxes,” which is obviously not Paraguay’s 10% IVA. I am no longer treating that line as a real tax. Another live rate for the same room showed $47 + $4.70, which is the sane 10% structure. Descanso del Mbocayaty shows $35 + $7 (20%), so I marked that one for direct verification too.
WhatsApp strategy: I intentionally do not tell them the OTA price first. The message asks for their best direct final price in guaraníes, including IVA and every charge, plus breakfast and payment method. That gives them room to quote lower instead of anchoring them to the platform price.
