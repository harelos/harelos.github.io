Straight answers

What the price does and does not mean.

Can I subscribe today?

No. Paid checkout is closed. The Windows pilot, account, license, and billing checks must pass before purchase opens.

Does the price include Claude or ChatGPT?

No. The user brings an eligible provider plan, subject to that provider’s limits and terms.

Is there a mobile app subscription?

No. The current phone-friendly surface is a browser planner. It does not sync live work with the desktop app.
