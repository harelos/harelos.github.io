Straight answers

Know exactly what this pilot does.

Do I need another AI subscription?

You need an eligible Claude or ChatGPT plan for the provider you connect. Foundry’s proposed monthly price is separate. Provider limits and any separately enabled API charges still apply.

Is the switch between providers automatic?

In the pilot, you approve a supported handoff. A provider switch may change capabilities or billing source, so Foundry does not promise identical output or uninterrupted work.

Is this the same as Harel’s private Foundry?

No. This commercial build is a separate product with its own local state. Private business connections and data are not packaged with it.

Can I buy Studio or Enterprise access now?

No. Studio and Enterprise are future plans and are not sold in this pilot. Multi-user access and hosted sync require separate security and release work.

What happens after I build the brief?

Export the mission file, then use it in the Windows desktop pilot after receiving access. The browser cannot run local agents or access your computer.
