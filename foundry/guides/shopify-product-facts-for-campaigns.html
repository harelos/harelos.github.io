Field guide / Brief the work

Collect product facts before writing a Shopify campaign

A product page is one source, but it may be out of step with the current offer, fulfillment rule, or approved claim language. Give a campaign team a dated product-facts sheet before asking it to write.

Practical guideFoundry editorial
Use this on a real job

Keep the current product, offer, sources, and decision owner beside the draft. The checklist works with any writing tool.

Capture the exact selling unit

Write the product, variant, size, pack quantity, price, currency, market, destination URL, and any discount condition. A campaign about a four-unit pack cannot borrow the image or price of a single unit. If a free gift appears only with one option, tie that condition to the option in the sheet.

Record shipping and guarantee language from the current store and policy, then confirm with the owner when those disagree. Do not ask an AI tool to choose the more attractive promise. The sheet should preserve the approved string and the date it was checked.

Add what the buyer needs to see

  • Current product photographs or video, with permission and exact variant mapping.
  • What the product does and how it is used, in words the brand has approved.
  • Known fit limits and common buyer questions.
  • Real reviews or support language with permission and context.
  • Any legal or policy restrictions on claims and comparisons.

Mark a claim’s source. A customer comment, supplier sheet, lab document, and product page are not interchangeable evidence. If the strongest creative idea depends on a fact you cannot verify, keep it as an open research item rather than writing around the gap.

Refresh the sheet before each launch

Prices, stock, shipping terms, and page URLs can change. Compare the sheet to the intended landing page and checkout path immediately before production. Save the checked date in the campaign pack. A short current sheet is more useful than a large stale brand document.
