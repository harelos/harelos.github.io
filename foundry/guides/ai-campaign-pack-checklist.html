Field guide / Brief the work

A reviewable AI campaign pack: the complete checklist

A campaign pack is useful when it makes a decision easier. It should let the operator compare ideas, inspect their evidence, and see what must happen before production.

Practical guideFoundry editorial
Use this on a real job

Keep the current product, offer, sources, and decision owner beside the draft. The checklist works with any writing tool.

Check the pack in the order you would use it

Open with an evidence brief, not the ad copy. It should state the product, buyer, offer, market, destination, approved facts, and unknowns. If the offer in the pack differs from the live page, stop there. A polished headline cannot rescue a mismatched price or promise.

Next, inspect the concepts. Each one needs a distinct reason the buyer might care. “Save time,” “quick results,” and “fast routine” may be the same concept. Distinct concepts might address different buyer jobs, decision barriers, or moments of use. Give each concept one sentence explaining why it deserves a test.

Minimum contents

  • One evidence brief: source-linked facts and clearly marked unknowns.
  • Three concepts: different reasons to buy, not three tone changes.
  • Copy for each concept: a short and a longer primary-text draft, plus headlines that match the idea.
  • One page outline: opening promise, proof sequence, objection handling, and action for the chosen concept.
  • Review notes: unsupported claims, missing creative assets, and decisions still owned by a person.

Count is only a completeness check. A concept can still be weak or repetitive after all fields are filled. A reviewer should compare the concepts side by side and ask whether each would teach the team something different if tested.

Use a simple acceptance test

Ask a second person to answer four questions without reading the prompt history: What is the current offer? What is each concept testing? Which claims have evidence? What can be approved today? If they need the chat transcript to answer, the pack is not a handoff yet.

Accept the file only as a planning result. Production, account access, and publishing are separate decisions. This boundary keeps a useful draft from being mistaken for an approved live campaign.
