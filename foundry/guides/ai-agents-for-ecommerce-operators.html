What should an AI team do for an ecommerce operator first?

An ecommerce operator has many possible AI tasks. The best first task is one with a recurring deadline, source material you can supply, and an output you can judge without giving an agent live account permissions.

Practical guideFoundry editorial
Use this on a real job

Keep the current product, offer, sources, and decision owner beside the draft. The checklist works with any writing tool.

Pick one repeatable decision

A campaign review pack is a useful candidate: the operator knows the product and current offer, has customer language or performance notes, and needs to decide which concept deserves production. The team can draft and review in local files. The result is inspectable even if the brand has no direct store integration.

Other jobs may fit later, but they require different data and trust boundaries. A store audit needs current catalog and analytics access. Customer service needs verified identity, policies, and channel permissions. Budget management needs reliable attribution, account scopes, and a rollback path. Treating all of these as one “AI ecommerce agent” makes the first promise too broad to prove.

A useful two-role pattern

Give the maker a narrow assignment: build three distinct campaign concepts from approved facts. Give the reviewer a different job: find unsupported claims, repeated angles, offer mismatches, and missing decisions. The operator then accepts or revises the combined file. A reviewer is valuable only when they challenge the output with the brief, not when they repeat the maker’s praise.

Measure the right first outcome

  • Was the final file complete enough to review without the chat history?
  • How many factual corrections did the operator need?
  • How much operator time did the task consume from brief to accepted file?
  • Did the selected concept keep the same promise on its proposed page?

These are pilot questions, not claims that AI improves revenue. The workflow earns a larger scope only after it reliably finishes the smaller one. Foundry’s current commercial pilot starts with that campaign job and leaves live store and ad actions outside it.

Put the method to work
