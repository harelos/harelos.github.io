Straight answers

Know exactly what this pilot does.

Will it connect my Shopify store and Meta account?

The first commercial pilot works from files and facts you choose. Direct Shopify and Meta account connections are planned, but they are not in this build. The app will not ask for store or ad credentials during this pilot.

Can it change a live ad budget?

No. This pilot prepares a campaign pack for review. It does not write to Meta or your live store.

Can I work from my phone?

The web mission planner works on a phone and exports a mission file. Agent execution and local files currently run in the Windows desktop app. Phone-to-desktop live sync is not in this build.

Can I buy Studio or Enterprise access now?

No. Studio and Enterprise are future plans and are not sold in this pilot. Multi-user access and hosted sync require separate security and release work.

What happens after I build the brief?

Export the mission file, then use it in the Windows desktop pilot after receiving access. The browser cannot run local agents or access your computer.
